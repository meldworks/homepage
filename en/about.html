About meldworks

MISSION

Know yourself, deeper.

meldworks is a team built around a single mission: to turn the state of the brain into everyday information. EEG has long been confined to specialized medical and research institutions.

By making brainwave measurement as simple as wearing an earphone, we are working to create a world where people can quietly observe their own state in daily life, and understand themselves more deeply.

VISION

"A society where understanding
your own state is everyday."
