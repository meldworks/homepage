HUSH

Wearable EEG

HUSH is one of the world's most compact wearable EEG devices, designed to measure brainwaves simply by being worn like an earphone. It quietly and continuously observes your inner state throughout daily life.

  • Ultra-compact design
  • Wireless and comfortable to wear
  • Real-time EEG measurement
  • App and analysis software integration (planned)
  • Designed for everyday wear
Request information
FEATURES

What makes HUSH different

FEATURE 01

Ultra-compact design

Conventional EEG required multiple electrodes across the entire head. HUSH consolidates this into a single point at the ear, redefining what daily-wear EEG can be.

FEATURE 02

Wireless & cable-free

Wires are a major barrier to everyday use. HUSH's wireless design allows free movement, enabling measurement in the natural flow of daily life.

FEATURE 03

Real-time measurement

Brainwave data is processed and transmitted in real time. Changes in focus, relaxation, and arousal can be observed at the moment they occur.
