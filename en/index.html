ABOUT

What is meldworks

meldworks Inc. is a brain-tech startup based in Tokyo. We are reinventing the EEG — once limited to hospitals and research labs — as a wearable device for daily life.

We start from a simple conviction: knowing the state of the brain can change the way people live. When stress, focus, fatigue, and arousal become accessible information, both individual performance and the shape of society will transform.

WHY BRAINWAVE

Why brainwaves?

The most essential biosignal

Brainwaves directly reflect thought, emotion, focus, fatigue, and arousal. They reveal inner states that heart rate or accelerometers simply cannot capture — in real time.

An untapped frontier

Within the wearable market, daily use of EEG remains an emerging field worldwide. The high technical barrier creates a strong first-mover advantage and an enormous societal upside.

The potential to change behavior

Beyond mere visualization, brainwave-based feedback and habit optimization can fundamentally reshape daily life — improving how people work, rest, and recover.

TECHNOLOGY

Our technical strengths

01

Ultra-compact design

Proprietary mounting and miniaturization that fits a high-precision EEG sensor into the limited space of the ear. Daily wearability is the starting point of every design decision.

02

Noise & signal processing

In daily environments, body movement, muscle activity, and electromagnetic noise contaminate signals. Our in-house algorithms remove them with high accuracy and extract clean brainwave signals.

03

Real-time analysis platform

A real-time pipeline that converts measured brainwaves into state estimates immediately, enabling low-latency feedback to the user.

04

Data utilization & behavior change

We are building a data foundation that learns individual brainwave patterns and develops it into optimization feedback for habits and condition management.
